About the Spoken Devotion Version (SDV)

The Spoken Devotion Version of the Bible is translated from the original Hebrew, Aramaic, and Greek texts using Grok Translate, then carefully edited for clarity and easy readability. It is an original devotional translation — not copied, adapted, or derived from any other published Bible translation.

Spoken Devotion is a personal creative ministry — not a registered church or nonprofit. Spoken Devotion is free to watch, listen to and share.

© 2026 Spoken Devotion LLC. All glory to God the Father. Support the ministry on Ko-fi
Audio tracks and voices are generated with Suno. Our Scripture readings come from a fresh translation developed with assistance from Grok, based on original language sources, and then carefully edited for devotional use.
